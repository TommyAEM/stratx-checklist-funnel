The 7-Gate Strategy Validation Checklist

  • GATE 1 Data integrity — real prices, real volume, no gaps
  • GATE 2 Sample size — enough trades to mean anything
  • GATE 3 Positive expectancy — after spreads and costs
  • GATE 4 Drawdown discipline — survivable losing runs
  • GATE 5 Parameter stability — plateaus, not lucky peaks
  • GATE 6 Monte Carlo robustness — reshuffle and stress it
  • GATE 7 Independent reproduction — out-of-sample proof
